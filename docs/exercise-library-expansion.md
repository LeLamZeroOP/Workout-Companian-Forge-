# Exercise library expansion

Reviewed 2026-10-16. The library contains 200 built-in exercises: the original 57 plus 143 common repetition-based movements. New entries include bodyweight, band, kettlebell, barbell, dumbbell, cable and machine exercises.

All 200 entries have a selected demonstration. Every unique selected source (185) played inside the app using the official YouTube IFrame API, with matching video ID, PLAYING state, no reported player error, prerecorded metadata and landscape intrinsic video dimensions. Full evidence is in [the coverage report](../output/library-video-coverage.json) and [playback records](../output/expansion-playback-evidence.json). Sources are available as checked on this date; availability can change.

Full tutorials of at least three minutes are preferred. Selected focused landscape lessons of at least 80 seconds have an explicit selection reason in the source data. Shared tutorials carry variation notes and published chapter starts where available. Portrait Shorts, microclips and sources that block embedding were replaced.

Existing saved definitions take precedence over built-ins. Opening the library does not rewrite saved mappings, programs or workout history. Saving a program adds only missing referenced definitions. Active-workout additions keep independent exercise snapshots.

Contributions and muscle roles are editable tracking estimates within the existing 13-muscle model. Kettlebells, trap bars, balls and ab wheels use Other equipment plus searchable names/aliases. Repetition-based bodyweight and band movements explain how to log added external load; unilateral movements explain the per-side convention. Timed holds and carries were excluded because the logger records repetitions.

The library initially shows 24 entries and exposes more in batches of 24. Search and equipment/muscle filters cover the complete library, including entries beyond the visible batch.
