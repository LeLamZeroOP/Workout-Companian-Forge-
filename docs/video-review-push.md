## Evidence and limitations

- Direct YouTube player metadata was inspected for the following selected IDs before parent-centralized verification began: t1iaVBMItPo (267 s), vcBig73ojpE (734 s), 0f6-uCUKqgA (451 s), gNBU7hmW2EU (186 s), H4mVGHaK2f4 (183 s), IODxDxX7oi4 (217 s), _RlRDWO2jfg (478 s), 7C-btvC-UX4 (456 s), v_ZkxWzYnMc (484 s), DvjmJdEswQ4 (670 s), GobPtFwc_Qk (826 s), 2dKBS61BX24 (626 s), fOHouR0t9Cw (263 s), 2z8JmcrW-As (278 s), and KvBMo1bF3Mk (200 s). At that check they returned playability status OK, playableInEmbed true and 1280×720 embed metadata. These are availability signals, not a guarantee about every browser/device/account.
- Remaining listed durations came from live YouTube long-form search results and need the parent's direct player verification. Single-arm cable press is explicitly provisional until duration/orientation verification; it is the one possible short landscape exception. Prioritize an expert teaching the exact unilateral movement over a long generic cable workout.
- The [Fit Father Project creator page](https://www.fitfatherproject.com/dumbbell-bench-press/) embeds t1iaVBMItPo and supplies accompanying step-by-step setup, pressing, lowering and breathing instructions. It is a useful printable companion to the 4:27 video.
- The [J2FIT standing press source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fOHouR0t9Cw) publishes chapters at 0:23 equipment setup, 0:43 execution, 1:28 form tips and 2:10 mistakes. It is a dedicated lesson, not a workout compilation.
- The [Barbell path shoulder-press source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7C-btvC-UX4) publishes bench-angle, setup, elbow, wrist-alignment and execution chapters. Its specificity is the reason to choose this over the old Howcast demonstration.
- The [new Colossus cable-fly source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hrh0K1Oo7Yc) explicitly describes cable-height variants and mistakes including excessive load, pressing rather than flying, changing elbow bend and shoulder takeover.
- The [retained Jonni Shreve face-pull source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DvjmJdEswQ4) publishes detailed technique chapters through real-time reps. Its 11:10 duration slightly exceeds the preferred range but is justified by its exact instructional focus.
- The [Gymless decline push-up source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5dEc154cCc) begins the elevated-feet setup at 1:45. That is the supplied startSeconds value. Basic execution precedes the other push-up variants beginning at 3:57. The app must preserve the note so users do not mistake later variations for the prescribed exercise.
- The converging incline selection is an exact Atlantis-machine review by Dr. Mike. It needs a final content-scope check because a product review can include less technique than a dedicated lesson. If it is unsuitable, consider [RP's dedicated machine technique lesson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SUdQ1roIWz0), 9:06, after verifying the relevant incline-machine section.
- The unilateral cable selection is from [Nick Tumminello's exact-exercise tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNUWfchnLIE). Strong alternate expert source: [Scott Herman's one-arm cable press](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uVmRqrTcHyc). Longer candidates [Claw Athletics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TXUIfaobJ_g), 4:34, and [Elevate Everyday](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EMONnG0ldxY), 5:44, were discovered but not accepted on runtime alone; the former's unilateral content and the latter's creator/production quality need confirmation.
